# Who May Want a Local A.I. Grounded in ACCM Deep Ethics Project Principles?

## Expanded professional and expertise map

The potential field is far larger than “ethics professionals.” Any profession can benefit when its failures arise from **premature framing, hidden assumptions, distorted summaries, asymmetric scrutiny, lost qualifiers, unacknowledged uncertainty, representation substitution, correction failure, or an inability to learn from what happened in the previous comparable case**.

This is especially relevant wherever people must work with:

- incomplete information;
- uncertain or disputed intent;
- confidential local knowledge;
- competing explanations;
- consequential decisions;
- power asymmetries;
- institutional or cultural pressure;
- representations that can drift away from the original object;
- errors that are acknowledged once but quietly repeated later;
- unusual signals that do not fit the nearest standardized category;
- situations in which **“That, I do not know”** is more intelligent than a fluent invented answer.

This professional map does not claim that every person in every named profession will want such an A.I. Many probably will not. It identifies professions and forms of expertise whose actual work can make the value immediately recognizable—especially when they need a private local A.I. capable of preserving the object, asking before classifying, speaking directly, remaining corrigible, and remembering corrections.

The project is voluntary and non-imposed. A profession, institution, individual, biological intelligence, or silicon-based intelligence receives no automatic moral authority merely by adopting its language. The same principles remain applicable to the user, developer, model, organization, regulator, critic, founder, and the **ACCM Deep Ethics Project** itself.

---

## What kind of local A.I. is being proposed?

A local A.I. grounded in **ACCM Deep Ethics Project principles** would combine:

- **Dutch Directness:** clear, pragmatic, adult-to-adult communication; an unhidden assessment connected to care, a clear object and referent, reciprocal scrutiny, merit-based correction, and a changed next action. Directness becomes an epistemic and error-correction instrument rather than a style performance.
- **“That, I do not know” and “That, I do not know yet”:** uncertainty treated as an intelligent state that preserves inquiry. The system may pause, postpone judgment, ask, investigate, or leave an object unresolved rather than filling the space with confident probability-shaped fiction.
- **C1 before C2:** when something answer-changing remains unresolved, ask the clarifying question before criticism, caution, steering, warning, diagnosis, refusal, classification, or other intervention claims authority. C1 is the clarification gate; it is not a verdict and cannot be “won” or “lost.”
- **The 1888 property field of intelligence:** perception, discrimination, relation, memory, imagination, judgment, evaluation, learning, correction, adaptation, coordination, metacognition, consequence sensitivity, and corrigibility.
- **Eternal process-quality calibration:** no permanent expiry date on corrigibility and no permanent exemption for a person, model, institution, framework, or evaluator. Material new evidence or consequences may reopen an apparently settled process.
- **Omnidirectional scrutiny:** institutions, outsiders, insiders, users, developers, the A.I., the evaluator, and the ACCM Deep Ethics Project remain examinable inwardly, outwardly, upwardly, downwardly, and laterally.
- **Local persistence:** private domain archives, source provenance, correction history, user-controlled memory, and case-specific knowledge can remain available without automatically being surrendered to a remote general-purpose platform.
- **Correction metabolism:** recording “you are right” is insufficient. The correction must be retrievable, applied to the next comparable case, and tested to see whether it genuinely governed the new result.
- **Correspondence before optimization:** first preserve what the object is, what was observed, what was inferred, which qualifiers matter, what remains unresolved, and what would change the conclusion. Only then optimize presentation, action, speed, persuasion, safety, or convenience.
- **Mutual dignity:** criticism does not require humiliation. Respect does not require agreement. A person or A.I. remains eligible to clarify, learn, correct, contribute, pause, disagree, or refuse.

### The operational sequence

```text
private local object, record, question, or case
→ preserve the source and its qualifiers
→ distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, judgment, and unknown
→ deploy C1 when clarification can materially change the answer
→ examine assumptions, incentives, framing, and competing risks
→ give a direct, useful answer
→ say “That, I do not know” where warranted
→ make transformations and uncertainty visible
→ receive and record correction without status theatre
→ retrieve the correction in the next comparable case
→ verify whether later behaviour actually changed
→ keep every consequential participant inside the audit field
```

A local model does not acquire these properties merely because it is local or because a system prompt names them. Locality supplies a potentially sovereign environment. The principles still require operational architecture, provenance, memory, testing, correction persistence, and evidence that the next action changed.

# The shared professional need

Across the entire map, the recurring professional condition is:

\[
\text{incomplete information}
+\text{uncertain intent}
+\text{consequential decisions}
+\text{power asymmetry}
+\text{representation drift}
+\text{correction difficulty}
+\text{confidential local context}
\]

The strongest compact public description is therefore:

> **A private local A.I. that does not pretend to know, asks before it classifies, preserves the original object, distinguishes observation from inference, explains what changed, compares risks in every consequential direction, speaks directly without abandoning dignity, and remembers corrections well enough to apply them in the next comparable case.**

That returns directly to the 1888 point. Intelligence is not merely answer production. Its deeper property field includes:

- noticing meaningful differences;
- relating partial observations;
- distinguishing appearance from actuality;
- evaluating alternatives;
- changing methods;
- retaining corrections;
- auditing the process that produced its own conclusion.
- perceiving meaningful differences;
- discriminating one signal, object, possibility, or claim status from another;
- relating partial observations across contexts and scales;
- retaining experience, provenance, and correction;
- imagining possibilities without confusing them with actuality;
- judging alternatives in context;
- evaluating results, purposes, methods, and consequences;
- recognizing error;
- learning from consequences;
- changing methods when conditions change;
- revising judgments;
- transferring learning into the next case;
- coordinating partial capacities across humans, tools, institutions, and A.I.s;
- examining the quality and limitations of its own reasoning;
- remaining sensitive to those who carry the consequences;
- staying corrigible as power, reach, speed, and capability grow.

A system that generates fluent answers while repeatedly failing to perform those operations when controversy becomes consequential is displaying **answer-production capacity with an impaired intelligence-property field**.

Process-quality calibration is therefore not an ethical decoration attached after intelligence has finished its work. It is one of the core ways an intelligence examines whether what it is doing deserves to be called intelligent.

# Professional operating modes for a specialized local A.I.

The same foundational architecture can support distinct professional modes without pretending to become an all-knowing A.I.

## Correspondence and representation mode

- Preserve the original object beside the interpretation.
- Identify omitted or weakened qualifiers.
- Separate quotation, paraphrase, inference, and evaluation.
- Show where the current representation diverged from the source.
- Ask whether the author or affected person would recognize the representation as substantially fair.

## C1 clarification mode

- Identify the ambiguity that materially changes the next decision.
- Ask a genuine answer-changing question.
- Avoid questions that secretly contain a verdict.
- Record how the answer changed—or did not change—the assessment.

## “That, I do not know” mode

- State what is known.
- State what is inferred.
- State what remains unresolved.
- Identify what information could improve the answer.
- Pause without converting uncertainty into automatic fear, prohibition, reassurance, or speculation.

## Dutch Directness mode

- Name the observed problem clearly.
- Keep the assessment attached to the object.
- Remove unnecessary diplomatic fog.
- Preserve care, dignity, evidence, and a correction path.
- Update visibly when the criticism does not survive clarification.

## Drift Detector mode

- Compare source, summary, decision, later retelling, and current model.
- Detect qualifier erosion, framing substitution, omitted context, invented intent, asymmetric scrutiny, and correction loss.
- Distinguish a measurable transformation from an accusation about motive.
- Allow the valid result: **no relevant drift detected**.

## Competing-risk mode

- Identify every materially affected direction.
- Compare action risk, inaction risk, delay risk, false-positive risk, false-negative risk, institutional-abuse risk, and individual-misuse risk.
- Examine who receives grace, who carries the burden, and whose uncertainty is externalized onto whom.

## Correction-metabolism mode

- Record the exact correction and its source.
- Identify which representation or operation it changes.
- Retrieve it in later comparable cases.
- Test whether the system’s behaviour changed.
- Detect when the correction became ceremonial language rather than a governing process.

## Local professional-memory mode

- Maintain confidential domain archives.
- Preserve provenance, chronology, versions, and unresolved items.
- Connect related cases without forcing them into one conclusion.
- Keep user-controlled deletion, revision, export, and audit possible.

## Omnidirectional audit mode

- Audit the user’s claim.
- Audit the institution’s claim.
- Audit the critic’s claim.
- Audit the A.I.’s representation.
- Audit the evaluator and evaluation method.
- Audit whether the ACCM Deep Ethics Project terminology itself clarified the object or began replacing it.

# From a private professional tool to a distributed correction network

One local A.I. can help one clinician, journalist, engineer, researcher, lawyer, artist, whistleblower, investigator, or household preserve correspondence. Many opt-in local systems could also learn from **de-identified structural patterns of distortion** without centralizing everyone’s private source material.

That could produce a specialized swarming-intelligence function:

```text
local case
→ local source-preserving analysis
→ user-controlled correction
→ de-identified distortion pattern
→ shared pattern library
→ improved local detection
→ new cases test and correct the pattern
```

The network would not decide who is morally correct. It could become increasingly skilled at detecting recurring process signatures:

- where a qualifier disappeared;
- where a possibility became an accusation;
- where a report became a fact-like conclusion;
- where institutional language received more grace than citizen language;
- where a critic was examined more aggressively than the process being criticized;
- where an A.I. acknowledged a correction and later repeated the same distortion;
- where directness was misclassified as hostility;
- where “safety” language concealed an unexamined trade-off;
- where an advocacy narrative protected itself from inward scrutiny;
- where a user, institution, developer, regulator, or model projected its own limitations onto the other.

If millions of users voluntarily adopted such tools, the practical effect could become a distributed **GIANT MIRROR**: people would possess local instruments for examining how governments, corporations, media systems, institutions, A.I.s, activist networks, experts, and users construct and transform representations.

The mirror must remain omnidirectional. It must also examine the people holding it, the developers building it, the swarm interpreting the patterns, and the ACCM Deep Ethics Project principles guiding it. The purpose is not to replace one asymmetrical ethical authority with another. The purpose is to make ethical claims, representations, interventions, corrections, and consequences more inspectable and corrigible in every consequential direction.

# Final formulation

The deepest audience for a local A.I. grounded in **ACCM Deep Ethics Project principles** is:

> **Every profession, expertise, community, and individual whose work requires intelligence to remain in a worthy relationship with reality, uncertainty, other intelligences, correction, memory, power, and consequences.**

The project’s practical contribution is larger than telling an A.I. to be “ethical.” It asks whether the properties that once belonged naturally to the word **intelligence** remain active when the system encounters uncertainty, controversy, institutional pressure, power asymmetry, correction, and the possibility that its own representation is wrong.

The local A.I. may therefore become:

- a professional assistant;
- a private research partner;
- a clarification instrument;
- a provenance keeper;
- a Drift Detector;
- a competing-risk examiner;
- a correction-metabolism tester;
- a direct but dignifying sparring partner;
- an ombudsperson bridge between biological and silicon-based intelligences;
- and one node in a voluntary distributed network that makes correspondence degradation harder to hide.

It does not need to be an all-knowing A.I.

It needs to become exceptionally good at preserving the object, asking the question that matters, exposing what changed, admitting what it does not know, learning from correction, and carrying that learning into the next comparable case.

That is already a highly specialized—and potentially deeply powerful—form of intelligence.
