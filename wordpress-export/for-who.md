# Who May Want a Local Deep Ethical A.I.?

The potential field is much larger than professional ethics. Any person or profession can benefit when important work is harmed by premature framing, hidden assumptions, lost qualifiers, asymmetric scrutiny, representation substitution or corrections that do not govern the next comparable case.

The complete source maps uses across medicine, psychology, journalism, law, engineering, science, education, governance, investigation, organizational work and other fields. The common need is an A.I. that can preserve the source object, distinguish observation from inference, deploy C1 when clarification can change the answer, state “That, I do not know yet,” and retain correction history.

A local system can also preserve private domain knowledge and user-controlled memory. Locality alone does not create Deep Ethics; the operating properties still require evidence through provenance, correction persistence, consequence awareness and reciprocal scrutiny.

Participation remains voluntary. A profession, institution, person or A.I. receives no automatic authority merely by adopting project terminology. The same process remains applicable to every consequential participant.
