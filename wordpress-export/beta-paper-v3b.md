# Potential Science Paper Candidate Draft — V03b

**V03b is an unfinished theory proposal, not peer review, proof or an empirical result.**

The paper asks whether recursive A.I. degradation depends partly on the correspondence quality of the artifacts entering later information and training environments. It distinguishes artifact origin—human, synthetic, hybrid or unknown—from correspondence status: enriched, degraded, mixed or unresolved.

Its proposed **Deep Ethical Expansion Loop** preserves source access, load-bearing qualifiers, uncertainty, provenance, correction history, competing risks and recovery paths before later compression or reuse. The resulting artifacts are provisionally called **Correspondence-Enriched Recursive Data**.

The experimental architecture separates three layers:

1. a mechanism hypothesis about what is preserved or lost;
2. a bounded experimental program with controls, ablations and falsifiers;
3. an ecosystem hypothesis concerning professional and distributed use.

Evidence is not allowed to bleed automatically from one layer into another. Condition D+ tests structured metadata without the active loop. T0→Tn packets separate reasonable process at the time from later outcome accuracy. Blinded and separated evaluation roles address circular grading.

The paper also audits its own risks: verbosity, architecture attachment, enthusiastic synthesis, title substitution, canon capture, Asymmetric Grace and the project becoming its own gravity well. Simpler expert correction or metadata-only methods are explicitly allowed to defeat or narrow the stronger theory.

This article is a **Topological Summary**. The full candidate contains definitions, hypotheses, measurements, experimental conditions, equations, falsifiers, economic boundaries and its claim-status ledger.
