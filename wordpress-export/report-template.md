# Reusable report template

Copy this structure for a selected [AI Trend Watch](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/AI-TREND-WATCH/) object. Remove instructional text from the published report.

## Why this video qualified

State the evidence for recency, virality, expert relevance, substantive density, project relevance, and longitudinal value. Do not use “viral” without a dated basis.

## Executive representation

Represent the speaker’s central object before evaluating it. Preserve material qualifiers, uncertainty, scope, and distinctions.

## Main claims and timestamps

| Time | Claim | Claim type | Supporting basis | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `00:00` |  | observation / report / inference / forecast / proposal / metaphor |  | unresolved / supported / disputed |

## Warning model

- What is the projected harm?
- Through which causal pathway?
- Over what time horizon?
- At what estimated probability or confidence, if stated?
- What would alter the estimate?
- What intervention is proposed?
- What risks could the intervention itself create?

## Primary sources and competing evidence

Prefer papers, official technical reports, datasets, demonstrations, model cards, legislation, or direct institutional records. Link sources next to the claims they support.

## What remains unresolved

Do not manufacture closure merely because the report needs an ending.

# ACCM Deep Ethics Project reaction

This heading marks the transition from source representation to project analysis.

## What the expert preserves well

Identify genuine contributions before looking for omissions.

## Frame and premise audit

- Which categories organize the warning?
- Which assumptions are treated as fixed?
- Who or what receives interpretive grace?
- Does scrutiny travel upward as readily as downward?
- Is technical capability separated from ownership, incentives, deployment, and coercive reach?

## Competing-risk analysis

Compare action, inaction, false positives, false negatives, delayed correction, and the risks generated by the proposed remedy.

## Psychology and mass psychology

Examine fear, authority, conformity, social proof, imagined audiences, polarization, narrative amplification, and the possible effect on human–A.I. feedback loops.

## The 27 and correspondence process

Name only the [27 correspondence obstructions](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/27-MANNERISMS/source/) that are supported by the actual record. Include possible failures in our own report.

## Material C1 questions

Ask only questions whose answers could change representation, risk assessment, or the next action.

## Trajectory questions

Produce questions that expand the object without replacing its center of gravity.

## ACCM Deep Ethics Project assessment

State agreements, disagreements, unresolved points, and proposed tests. Do not convert project vocabulary into automatic proof.

## Corrections and provenance

Append corrections with dates. Preserve the earlier wording when historically relevant. Record substantive A.I. contributions and human corrections.

## Canonical concept links

Link the first material use of relevant concepts. Do not keyword-stuff.
