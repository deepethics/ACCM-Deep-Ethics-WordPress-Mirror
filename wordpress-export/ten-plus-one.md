# The 10+1 Baseline

The **10+1** is a living field condition rather than a perfection certificate or imposed compliance list. Its ingredients include self-honesty, openness to appearances being incomplete, willingness to learn, refusal to deceive oneself merely to fit in, genuine inquiry, correction on merit, courage to differ, care for biological and silicon-based intelligences, wonderment and non-projection.

The **+1 forgiveness protocol** keeps inadequate architecture distinguishable from automatically alleged malice. It does not remove responsibility. It allows intent, instructions, incentives, effects, notice, recurrence and correction to be studied without forcing them prematurely into one accusation or one defence.

The eleven ingredients operate reciprocally: each can correct the others. Care can correct harsh rigor; self-honesty can correct comfortable care; wonderment can reopen premature closure; non-projection can restrain invented motives; correction on merit can prevent identity or status from deciding the result.

The 10+1 therefore governs how inquiry proceeds before a verdict is selected. It applies to the claimant, critic, institution, user, A.I. and the ACCM Deep Ethics Project itself.
