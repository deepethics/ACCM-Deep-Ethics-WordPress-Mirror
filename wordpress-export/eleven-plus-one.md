## Two axes

**Topological home** and **evidentiary status** are independent.

A concept can have an obvious place in the map while its strongest causal or intentional wording remains unestablished. Parking a hypothesis inside a cluster does not promote it into a load-bearing wall.

Claim type on the register below is about **the wording of that numbered item**, not about whether a neighboring literature exists.
