# Published reports

> **No report has yet passed selection and publication review.**

This archive will contain the dated reports selected through the public [AI Trend Watch methodology](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/AI-TREND-WATCH/methodology/).

Each entry will preserve:

- its original publication date and source snapshot;
- the evidence-based video report;
- the separate ACCM Deep Ethics Project reaction;
- predictions and warnings suitable for later review;
- corrections, status changes, and later vindication or contradiction.

The archive will remain deliberately small. An empty archive is preferable to filling it with material that is merely recent, famous, alarming, or algorithmically popular.
