# Δ Processing — What Changed?

**BETA — source-based editorial synthesis**

Δ Processing makes transformations available for comparison. Within this network, the question concerns what changed in the object’s representation, the governing process, or what became possible next.



## Distinguish the changes

| Change | What a record can preserve |
|---|---|
| Representation | Which claim, condition, relation, or qualifier changed. |
| Process | Which next move changed after a correction. |
| Relation | Which connection between concepts became visible. |
| Uncertainty | What remains open or now has a different evidential status. |
| Persistence | Whether the change remains relevant later. |

This is an editorial recording aid. The categories do not replace the larger observation field.

## Intent, responsibility, and subsequent choices

John's accepted [6 INTENT MECHANICS](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#six-intent-mechanics) extend this comparison to instructions, incentives, mission/output discrepancies, risk management, reasoning assumptions, and choices. The [intent Δ record](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#intent-delta-processing) preserves purpose, conditions, choice, consequence, feedback, and later choice. The [responsibility inquiry](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/NETWORK/intent-resonator/#responsibility) asks whether acknowledgment results in repair and lasting correction. These are labeled editorial applications of his questions.

## Δ and the Outnuancing Network

A new phrase may make a relation easier to retrieve. The relevant question is what its use contributes: a better question, a recovered condition, a discriminating test, a revision, or another inspectable change. The seven-vector proposal connects meaning and use; the correction record asks what actually occurred.

## Triad extension — three deltas and an interaction term

The October 17 triad expands a later comparison beyond a single subtraction:

```text
Δ_observed = Δ_input + Δ_process + Δ_observer-adaptation + Δ_interaction
```

This is a conceptual accounting relation, not a claim that the terms are already measured on one common numeric scale. Its job is to prevent a changed result from being attributed entirely to “better processing” when the input also changed, or entirely to new evidence when the method and observer changed too.

No material change is a permitted delta result. Full provenance: [triad field note](https://deepethics.github.io/ACCM-Deep-Ethics-Project/AI-CONTRIBUTIONS/2026-10-17-triad-topological-awareness/).
